================================================================================
ADDITIONAL METRICS CALCULATED
================================================================================

Beyond standard metrics, the script now calculates:

1. Cohen's Kappa Score
   - Inter-rater agreement measure
   - Accounts for chance agreement

2. Matthews Correlation Coefficient
   - Balanced measure for binary classification
   - Works well with imbalanced datasets

3. Hamming Loss
   - Average fraction of labels incorrectly predicted

4. Jaccard Score
   - Intersection over union measure

5. Per-Class Metrics
   - Precision, Recall, F1 for each class
   - Support (number of samples)

6. Macro vs Weighted Averages
   - Comparison of averaging methods
   - Better understanding of model performance
